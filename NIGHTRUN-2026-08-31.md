## THE DEFECT — a false claim the product printed about its own citation

Four surfaces printed: *"No published figure prices an account wall separately.
It closes the same door as a CAPTCHA, so ReadyCounter charges it the same 24
points and says so."*

**I fetched the cited page myself before touching anything** — raw HTML via
`curl`, not a summary:

- URL: `https://presenc.ai/research/agent-cart-abandonment-statistics-2026`
- Read: **2026-08-31**, page states "Last updated: June 2026"

The "Causes of Agent Cart Abandonment" table has **six** rows, not two:

| Cause | Share | Fixable by merchant |
|---|---|---|
| Stale price or stock data at checkout | 26% | Yes |
| Captcha or verification wall | 24% | Yes |
| Price mismatch vs listed feed | 18% | Yes |
| **Required account or login** | **15%** | Yes |
| Unsupported payment method | 11% | Yes |

They sum to 100%. **CONFIRMED**: the exact figure the product said did not exist
was row four of the table the 26 and the 24 were lifted from. `research.md` had
reproduced **two** of six rows, so the gap the sentence reasoned from was one we
had made ourselves.

## SECOND DEFECT, found by the rebalance — and it was pre-existing in shape

`computeReadinessChecks` passed raw point totals built from **literals copied out
of the weight table**: `20 * (withGtin / total)`, `10 * (inStock / total)`. When
the weights moved to 14 and 7, those literals stayed at 20 and 10, and
`Math.min(w.max, …)` **clamped 17.5 down to a perfect 14/14** on a catalog that
was 88% identified. A wrong number wearing a right number's clothes; nothing went
red. Caught only because I dumped the real lines instead of trusting my
arithmetic.

Fixed structurally: `line()` now takes a **fraction 0..1** and applies the weight
in exactly one place, so a call site cannot hold a stale copy of a weight. Plus a
new assertion: a line scored on a partial ratio must print less than its full
weight.

Also fixed: `verify-stores.mjs` hardcoded `TOOL_COUNT = 13` while the product
registers 16 — harmless only because both clear the floor of 6, which is exactly
how a stale constant survives. It now reads `WEBMCP_TOOL_COUNT`.
