## SECOND DEFECT, found by the rebalance — and it was pre-existing in shape

`computeReadinessChecks` passed raw point totals built from **literals copied out
of the weight table**: `20 * (withGtin / total)`, `10 * (inStock / total)`. When
the weights moved to 14 and 7, those literals stayed at 20 and 10, and
`Math.min(w.max, …)` **clamped 17.5 down to a perfect 14/14** on a catalog that
was 88% identified. A wrong number wearing a right number's clothes; nothing went
red. Caught only because I dumped the real lines instead of trusting my
arithmetic.

Fixed structurally: `line()` now takes a **fraction 0..1** and applies the weight
in exactly one place, so a call site cannot hold a stale copy of a weight. Plus a
new assertion: a line scored on a partial ratio must print less than its full
weight.

Also fixed: `verify-stores.mjs` hardcoded `TOOL_COUNT = 13` while the product
registers 16 — harmless only because both clear the floor of 6, which is exactly
how a stale constant survives. It now reads `WEBMCP_TOOL_COUNT`.
