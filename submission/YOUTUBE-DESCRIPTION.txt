ReadyCounter — WebMCP Challenge

Paste your store. See what shopping agents can't read.

We asked 148 real DTC storefronts for their catalogue the way a shopping agent
asks. 48 answered and sent no catalogue. 22 refused the request outright — a
real browser couldn't get in either, so we don't score them. Of everything we
could read, not one store exposed a barcode an agent can match to a product.
Average coverage: 0%.

Eleven brands do publish barcodes — to Shopify's Catalog protocol — while their
own storefront hides them. Your shopper's agent reads the storefront.

The audit ends in a download, not advice: a CSV in Shopify's own import shape,
pre-filled with your product handles, with the barcode column blank where it's
missing. Fill it, re-import, re-audit, and we print the delta. We never ask for
write access to your catalogue.

The readiness score makes no model calls. It's arithmetic over a crawl, checked
against published research, so it's reproducible and can't be talked into a
better number. The model is the shopper, never the judge.

Sources: Presenc AI (agent cart abandonment), Adobe Digital Insights (AI traffic
conversion), Shopify Enterprise and Shopify's Q1 2026 earnings call, YouGov.
